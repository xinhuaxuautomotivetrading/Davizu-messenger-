Davizu Messenger Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Your phone number, name, optional profile photo and "about" text; the messages, photos, videos, voice notes and documents you send; statuses you post; and your block, report and privacy settings.

How we use it

To create your account, deliver your messages to the people you chat with, show your statuses to your contacts, and keep the service safe (for example, reviewing reports of abuse). We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

Messages are visible to you and the people in that chat. Your name, photo and about text are visible to other Davizu users who look up your number. Statuses are visible to people you have a chat with, for 24 hours. Files you send are stored with a private, hard-to-guess link, but anyone who has the link can open the file. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and file storage.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, turn off read receipts, block and report users, and delete messages for everyone, all inside the app. You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Delete my account, which removes your profile, messages and statuses from our database.

Children

Davizu is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
